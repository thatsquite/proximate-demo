01

Capital is moving

Official development assistance is shrinking and shifting toward instruments that reward collaboration. Grant makers increasingly expect co-investment, including participation from companies.

02

Compliance is tightening

EUDR, CSRD, and CSDDD create new requirements for companies sourcing from smallholders, while 40–67% of major trader supply remains untraceable.

03

Investors need local partners

Development finance institutions, climate funds, and impact investors need credible partners to originate opportunities, manage technical-assistance facilities, and deliver in frontier markets.

Proximate brings what these shifts demand: local presence, institutional relationships, cross-sector expertise, and a commercial approach to turning opportunity into action.
